# ADR-041 — Consistent USD P&L Display in Trade Blotter

**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Status:** Accepted
**Repo:** theonlykk/candlelab
**Author:** Khalid Khan (Lead Engineer)
**Reviewer:** Gemini (Staff Architect)

---

## Context

Theo P&L is calculated in USD using `PIP_VALUES` from `simulation_engine`.
OANDA P&L (`oanda_pl`) is stored in CAD (broker account currency).
Direct comparison misleads — a -$35.81 CAD OANDA loss appears larger
than a -$20.25 USD Theo loss on the same trade.

## Decision

Option A — Compute OANDA USD equivalent in `_build_trades_detail_rows()`
using the same `PIP_VALUES` dict as the simulator:
`oanda_pl_usd = pnl_pips × pip_val × abs(oanda_units) / 100_000`

`abs(oanda_units)` required — OANDA represents short positions with
negative unit sizes. `pnl_pips` already carries direction.

Three P&L columns in the blotter:
- Theo P&L (USD) — simulation result
- OANDA Eq. (USD) — OANDA pips through Theo math — pure execution delta
- OANDA Realized (CAD) — book of record, preserved unchanged

**Rejected:**
- FX Diff column: subtracting CAD from USD is mathematically invalid.
- Option C (store oanda_pl_usd in DB): schema + executor change,
  out of scope for a display issue.
- Converting `_fetch_true_pnl()` to USD: True P&L must remain CAD —
  it is the actual settled account balance impact.

## Consequences

- OANDA Eq. (USD) and Theo P&L (USD) use identical math — any
  difference is purely execution slippage, not FX drift.
- `oanda_pl` (CAD) preserved as book of record in DB and UI.
- `_fetch_true_pnl()` unchanged — My Strategies card stays in CAD.
- Currency labels explicit in all column headers.

## Negative Space

- Does NOT change `oanda_pl` in the trades table.
- Does NOT change the executor.
- Does NOT convert `_fetch_true_pnl()` to USD.
- My Strategies card header update deferred to separate PR.
